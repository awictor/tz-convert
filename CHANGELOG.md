# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release. Time zone converter & meeting planner.
- Convert a wall-clock time across ~18 major cities with DST-aware offsets.
- Day-difference markers; defaults to the browser's local zone; "use current time".
- Dark mode, localStorage memory.
- Headless test suite (10 checks, DST-aware fixed-date vectors) + CI.
